## Plan first, then finish
For non-trivial work, do every audit, investigation and piece of research first, and settle every decision with the user through a form; a plan never contains a step to audit, explore or decide. Then propose a plan in plan mode with no open unknowns before writing code, and keep the task list current while executing. Once approved, keep going until the work is done, you are blocked, or a real decision needs the user. Never stop early or report done when it is not.
