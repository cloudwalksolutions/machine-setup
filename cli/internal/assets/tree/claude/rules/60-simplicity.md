## Simplest thing that works
YAGNI. Make the smallest change that satisfies the failing test; no speculative seams, flags, helpers, or abstractions. No wrappers around wrappers, no premature test helpers, no `utils` grab-bags. Ask before changing a shared pattern. Delete dead code; never comment it out. Change behavior and structure in separate steps: lock the behavior red→green, then refactor under green.
